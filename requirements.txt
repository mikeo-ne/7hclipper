## How It Works

1. **Fetch**: YouTube video metadata and transcript are extracted using `yt-dlp`
2. **Analyze**: Transcript is analyzed for "best moments" using heuristics based on sentence structure, keyword density, and impact words
3. **Clip**: Identified moments are clipped and reformatted to vertical 9:16 aspect ratio using FFmpeg
4. **Caption**: Transcript segments are burned into the video as burnt-in subtitles
5. **Output**: Vertical short-form video with captions ready for TikTok/Reels/Shorts
